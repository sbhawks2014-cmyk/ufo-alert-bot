# UAP Verification Analyst Bulletin: September 1, 2021 Reports

**Executive Summary:** Analysis of incoming raw reports from September 1, 2021, highlights a mix of automated sensor network triggers, fairground aerial display misidentifications, high-altitude cylindrical ("Tic-Tac") visual reports, and low-altitude multi-colored light anomalies near airport airspace.

---

### Sighting Reports

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Eau Claire, WI | Multi-Colored "Flying Christmas Tree" (Stacked Lights)
  * **Summary:** A witness near Chippewa Valley Airport observed a bright, multi-colored light array that executed rapid vertical and horizontal maneuvers before hovering and vanishing. Despite the witness's familiarity with local aviation, the light maneuvers and stacked configuration strongly point to an uncoordinated custom drone flight or light rig.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 6/10
  * **Identification Flag:** Drone
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164588](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164588.html)

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Newington, CT | Undetermined (Automated Sensor Event)
  * **Summary:** An automated MADAR (Node 106) anomaly detection system triggered a brief log alert at 21:30 local time. Without an accompanying visual witness description, meteorological correlation, or secondary data, the physical nature of the trigger remains unknown.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 2/10
  * **Identification Flag:** Unexplained Anomaly
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164835](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164835.html)

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Hodgkins, IL | White "Tic-Tac" / Cylindrical
  * **Summary:** An observer reported three high-altitude white cylindrical objects moving slowly in loose formation through clear daytime skies over Chicago-area airspace before abruptly disappearing. While consistent with classic "Tic-Tac" descriptions, high-altitude research balloons or solar glare off commercial traffic cannot be ruled out.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 5/10
  * **Identification Flag:** Unexplained Anomaly
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164495](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164495.html)

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Salem, OR | Six Objects / Variable Formations (Lines, Circle, Arrow)
  * **Summary:** Oregon State Fair attendees observed six objects transitioning through deliberate geometric formations as they drifted west over a five-minute period. Given the location, timing, and structured movement, the objects were almost certainly a coordinated skydiving exhibition team or a cluster of fairground balloons.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 4/10
  * **Identification Flag:** Aircraft
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164496](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164496.html)

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Eads, TN | Undetermined (Automated Sensor Event)
  * **Summary:** An automated signal detection alert was logged by MADAR Node 32 during early morning hours. Lacking visual reports, radar cross-checks, or context, the record functions strictly as an isolated electromagnetic/sensor anomaly readout.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 2/10
  * **Identification Flag:** Unexplained Anomaly
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164847](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164847.html)
