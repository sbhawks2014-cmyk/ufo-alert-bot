# 🛸 UAP/UFO Verification Bulletin: September 1, 2021

**Headline:** Multi-Region Activity Highlights Daylight "Tic-Tac" Sightings in Illinois, Formation Swarms over Oregon, and Dual Automated MADAR Alerts

---

### Sighting Reports

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Eau Claire, WI | Stacked Multi-Colored Lights ("Flying Christmas Tree")
  * **Summary:** A resident near Chippewa Valley Airport observed a bright object perform rapid directional and altitude changes over an eight-minute duration. The object featured multi-colored, stacked lights that suddenly extinguished in mid-air.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 6/10 (Detailed report from an experienced local resident, though lacking photographic confirmation).
  * **Identification Flag:** Drone / Custom Light Array
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164588](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164588.html)

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Newington, CT | N/A (Automated Sensor Alert)
  * **Summary:** An automated MADAR (Multiple Anomaly Detection & Automated Recording) Node 106 logged an anomalous reading at 21:30 local time. No visual confirmation or observational narrative accompanied the system trigger.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 3/10 (Automated alert with no supporting telemetry or visual verification provided).
  * **Identification Flag:** Unexplained Anomaly (Sensor Alert)
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164835](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164835.html)

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Hodgkins, IL | Tic-Tac (Capsular / Cylindrical)
  * **Summary:** A witness observed two high-altitude white "tic-tac" shaped objects moving extremely slowly across a clear daylight sky before both disappeared simultaneously after four minutes. Minutes later, a third identical object was briefly spotted moving toward Chicago before vanishing.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 5/10 (Clear visual description referencing landmarks, but lacks secondary witness statements or photos).
  * **Identification Flag:** Unexplained Anomaly
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164495](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164495.html)

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Salem, OR | Variable Formation Swarm (Lines, Circle, Arrow)
  * **Summary:** Attendees at the Oregon State Fair spotted six objects in the northern sky transitioning between random, linear, circular, and arrow formations while drifting west. The maneuverability and fair venue strongly suggest organized aerial performers, skydivers, or a drone swarm demonstration.
  * **Credibility Score (1-10):** 7/10 (Daylight sighting with multiple observers noting distinct geometric structural changes).
  * **Identification Flag:** Aircraft (Skydivers / Performers) or Drone
  * **Evidence / Media:** No direct media attached.
  * **Original Link:** [NUFORC Report S164496](http://www.nuforc.org/webreports/reports/164/S164496.html)

* **Location & Observed Shape:** Eads, TN | N/A (Automated Sensor Alert)
